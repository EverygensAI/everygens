# Photo-to-short exercise: a curtain in morning light

This is an original planning exercise, not a generation-tested result. Prepare your own photograph of a curtain beside a closed window. Keep the frame uncluttered and leave room for a small movement.

Open [Everygen Studio](https://www.everygens.com/everygen-studio) and select a supported image-to-video workflow. Check the selected model's input role, controls, and current estimate before submitting.

## Starter brief

> Use the photograph as the visual starting point. The curtain makes one small, gentle sway, then becomes still. Keep the window frame, wall, light direction, and room layout consistent with the photograph. Stationary camera, one continuous view, no scene change.

## What to inspect

- Does the window remain straight through the clip?
- Does the curtain move without merging into the wall?
- Does the movement settle before the clip ends?
- Does the lighting stay consistent?

If the room changes, simplify the motion and keep the camera stationary. If the action is too subtle, describe one slightly clearer sway rather than adding several new events. Change one instruction at a time.
