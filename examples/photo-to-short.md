# Assignment: a quiet morning post

**Creative exercise:** no generated result is attached, and these directions have not been generation-tested.

Imagine a short post that pairs a morning photograph with a single sentence. The photograph supplies the atmosphere; the final caption supplies the message. Use your own image of a curtain beside a window, with an uncluttered area available for the caption.

## Choose the editorial treatment

| Treatment | Intended effect | Finishing decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Still cover | Give the sentence time to register. | Place the caption where it does not cover the window or curtain. |
| Gentle animated background | Keep a little activity behind the same message. | Bring in the caption after the opening has established the room. |

The image may already work as a still. For an animated version, open [Everygen Studio](https://www.everygens.com/everygen-studio) and choose a supported image-to-video workflow. Check its upload requirements and estimate.

## Direction for the animated version

> Preserve the room shown in the photograph. A slight movement passes through the curtain and settles. Keep the window, wall, and light direction steady. Leave the uncluttered area free of new objects. Keep the camera fixed and the view continuous.

## Judge it as a post

Watch the clip with the intended caption placed over it in your editor. Is the movement quiet enough to leave the sentence readable? Does the frame still look coherent when the caption disappears? Are the window edges stable, and can the ending be trimmed naturally?

If the animation competes with the message, a smaller movement or the still version may suit this particular post better. Treat motion as an editorial choice, not a requirement.
