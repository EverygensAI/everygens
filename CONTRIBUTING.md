# Contributing to the public guides

Focused corrections are welcome: clarify a step, repair a link, explain an input role, or improve an example's review criteria.

Before proposing a change:

1. Identify the document and the reader's problem.
2. Explain the proposed wording and why it helps.
3. Check relative document links and destination URLs.
4. Label planning exercises separately from tested results.
5. Include only material you are entitled to share.

Do not add production configuration, credentials, private records, or internal operational reports. Avoid fixed price or model-capability claims without current evidence. Keep the English and Chinese overview links aligned when changing navigation.

For product feedback, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/EverygensAI/everygens/issues). For private matters, follow [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
