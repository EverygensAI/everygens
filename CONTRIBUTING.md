# Improve a reader's route through the guides

Useful contributions help someone choose an entry point, understand a form, or finish a piece of content. A clarification about where caption editing happens can be more useful than a longer list of prompts.

## Describe the gap

Name the document, the reader's starting material, and the decision that remains unclear. Propose a focused change and explain how it resolves that gap. Link to the relevant public workspace when describing its controls.

For creative assignments, state whether the material is an untested proposal or an observed result. If you report an actual test, describe its settings and observations without presenting the outcome as universal. Share only material you are entitled to publish.

## Check a proposed edit

Open relative document links, verify destination URLs, and keep navigation consistent between the English and Chinese overviews. Use the live product for changing model, price, or plan details. Leave application configuration, access credentials, customer records, and internal reports out of this collection.

Submit a focused pull request or [documentation suggestion](https://github.com/EverygensAI/everygens/issues). Account questions follow the route in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
